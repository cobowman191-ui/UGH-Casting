Unofficial Grab’s Hardest

Grab has a ridiculous number of challenge maps and everybody has a different idea of which one is the worst. This is our list. We look at how many people who tried a map actually beat it, how long it takes, and how many checkpoints it gives you, then argue about the order. Nothing to do with Grab itself.

Changes only save in this browser. To make them public, download data.json and upload it next to index.html.

      How difficulty works: each map gets a number out of 10 from three things, how few checkpoints it has, how few players beat it, how few have beaten it for the time it has been out, and (a lot less) how long it takes. A 10/10 would be no checkpoints, an hour or more to beat, and nobody finishing it. The order on the page is still set by hand.

      Fan project. The maps and pictures belong to the people who made them, and to Grab.
